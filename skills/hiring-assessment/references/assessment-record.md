## Requirement and evidence

**Requirement:** independently coordinate service incidents and communicate recovery plans.

**Source evidence:** CV, Experience / ExampleCo, second bullet: describes coordinating three teams during service disruption and introducing customer updates. Label: described ownership; unverified CV claim.

**Interpretation:** relevant incident-coordination experience. The CV does not establish how decisions were made or whether recovery improved. Missing measurements remain unknown.

**Interview probe:** walk through one incident, your own decisions, the trade-offs, communications and evidence that recovery was sustained. What would you change?

**Recruiter reconciliation:** overview says there is no incident experience. That conflicts with the cited CV example; investigate depth rather than repeat the overview's conclusion.

## Separate activities, attribution and uncertainty

Synthetic source S002, Experience / ExampleCo: “Implemented queue consumers; the team reduced failures by 40%. Helped migrate billing APIs.”

- “Implemented queue consumers”: **described delivery**. The activity is specific even though design ownership, release responsibility and outcome are unknown.
- “The team reduced failures by 40%”: a separately attributed, unverified **team outcome**. Ask about baseline, measurement and the person's contribution; do not label it personal ownership.
- “Helped migrate billing APIs”: **unclear** personal activity. Ask which task or decision was theirs. This differs from no migration statement being present.
- If a confirmed second version says 15% instead of 40%, preserve both source references and ask whether periods or measures differ. Do not choose one figure or infer dishonesty.

## Candidate-level conclusion

**Role suitability:** relevant evidence worth validating at interview.
**Level evidence:** cross-team coordination is described; independence and scope need validation against the advertised expectations.
**Eligibility:** a mandatory professional qualification in the brief is not evidenced. Ask recruiting to determine eligibility; do not equate this uncertainty with lack of delivery capability or waive the requirement.
**Next step for hiring-manager review:** clarify eligibility and, if satisfied, interview on incident ownership and the other material role requirements. This is not a final hiring decision.

## When information is missing

- Without a target role or level, produce a sourced evidence summary and ask for the missing role/level; defer suitability and grade recommendations. Do not infer a Senior role from the current candidate folder or from tenure.
- Without repository context, assess the supplied requirements directly. Do not request a repository for a role that does not need one.
- Without a relevant playbook, identify the missing interview guidance or propose a clearly labelled structure; do not silently use the AI engineering pilot.
- Without interview observations, retain CV evidence labels and validation questions. Do not award interview-performance ratings from the CV.
