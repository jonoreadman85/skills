## When information is missing

- Without a target role or level, produce a sourced evidence summary and ask for the missing role/level; defer suitability and grade recommendations. Do not infer a Senior role from the current candidate folder or from tenure.
- Without repository context, assess the supplied requirements directly. Do not request a repository for a role that does not need one.
- Without a relevant playbook, identify the missing interview guidance or propose a clearly labelled structure; do not silently use the AI engineering pilot.
- Without interview observations, retain CV evidence labels and validation questions. Do not award interview-performance ratings from the CV.
